# New design philosophy

One sentence should drive every design decision:

> **"Explore my universe of systems."**

Not

> "Welcome to my portfolio."

# Motion

Minimal.

Everything should feel like space.

Not gaming.

Not cyberpunk.

Examples:

* stars drift slowly
* camera parallax
* planets rotate gently
* cards float 3px
* shooting star every 20-40 seconds
* constellation lines fade in

Visitors shouldn't notice the animation itself. They should notice how smooth the experience feels.

# Color palette

Keep it restrained:

* Background: `#050505`
* Surface: `#111111`
* Border: `rgba(255,255,255,.08)`
* Text: `#F5F5F5`
* Accent: `#6A7CFF` or a muted electric blue

Reserve color for interaction. When everything glows, nothing stands out.

## One final suggestion

The biggest change I'd make isn't visual. It's structural.

You're not a student showing coursework anymore. You're accumulating **artifacts**: production websites, AI systems, architecture documents, research papers, experiments, and startup ideas. Treat the portfolio as an archive of those artifacts.

If someone spends five minutes on the site, they should leave thinking:

> "This person doesn't just write code. They investigate problems, design systems, and ship them."

That's a much stronger impression than simply looking like another polished frontend developer.
